## What is this blog for?

I will write about nothing but hardware and logical approaches to software in regards to hardware.


## Background

Growing up I always waited for the next game to come out and I was just amazed at what people could do with some hardware and some software. I saw also some ways where people could be helped in regards to medical devices, self care and tools to help keep them safe.
The sky wasn't the limit and as a programmer you could do anything. I played a lot of games and became interested in programming when I wanted to make my own game. I have been programming ever since.
